**Fan-in guard (lane-agnostic):** after ANY fan-out, reconcile expected-vs-actual completions before synthesizing or reviewing the merged result — count returns against launches and surface the gap, never build on a silently partial set. Subagents can die with no failure notification in any lane (the k3 silent-403 scar — 6 of 10 died, only 2 reported — is the local proof, not a Kimi-only quirk); a report assembled from partial returns looks complete and isn't. For wide fan-outs, layer the fan-in: batch the results, summarize per batch, then combine summaries — never pour the raw pile into one merge step and blow the merging agent's context.

**Leaf run ledger (2026-07-28):** liveness is a ledger, not a memory. Every bash-launched leaf (codex exec, headless claude/kimi wrappers) is launched through the `leaf-run` wrapper (`~/.config/claude-code/bin/leaf-run`), which records launch and completion in the machine-local registry (`~/.claude/leaf-registry.jsonl`); `leaf-sweep` reconciles it — a start with no completion and a dead PID is an 🚧 INTERRUPTED candidate (ack it with `leaf-sweep --ack` after posting the bus comment), a live PID is the in-flight set. Run `leaf-sweep` at session start (it feeds the INTERRUPTED sweep), before any logout/shutdown (the login-session mortality check), and after bash-lane fan-outs as the mechanical form of the fan-in guard. Harness-tracked subagents (Agent tool) are ledgered by the harness already and skip the wrapper.
